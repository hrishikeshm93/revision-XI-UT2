Formula Vault

Circular Motion

Angular Speed $\omega = \frac{\Delta\theta}{\Delta t} = 2\pi f$
Linear Speed $v = \omega r$
Centripetal Acc. $a_c = \frac{v^2}{r} = \omega^2 r$

Laws of Motion

Newton's 2nd Law $\vec{F}_{net} = \frac{d\vec{p}}{dt} = m\vec{a}$
Friction $f_{s,max} = \mu_s N, \quad f_k = \mu_k N$
Banked Road (Max Safe) $v_{max} = \sqrt{rg \left(\frac{\mu_s + \tan\theta}{1 - \mu_s \tan\theta}\right)}$

Work, Energy, Power

Work Done (Constant F) $W = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{d} = Fd \cos\theta$
Work Done (Variable F) $W = \int_{x_i}^{x_f} F(x) dx$
Work-Energy Theorem $W_{net} = \Delta K = K_f - K_i$
